FLIGHT MANUAL

Small crystals.
Big word energy.

  1. Open & collect. Aim light pulses at crystals. Fly close to pick up the letters. The opener and their teammates get a 2-second collection priority.
  2. Build & score. Carry up to 10 letters. Make a 3–10 letter English word. Only used letters leave your tray. Rare letters and longer words score more.
  3. Find your moment. Build mode stops your ship and gives up to 8 seconds of protection. Submitting, canceling, or timing out ends it; the cooldown is 15 seconds. You cannot collect or pulse while protected.
  4. Outspell your crew. Highest score when the clock ends wins. A word scores once per player per match. Teams add their players' points. Ties share the win.
Disruption, when enabled

Three rival pulse contacts reset your ship for 3 seconds and drop up to 3 letters. Points stay. Dropped letters are immediately shared. Your ship returns with brief protection. Friendly fire is off.

Controls: WASD/arrows + mouse on computers. Two touch sticks on phones. Space opens word building; Enter submits; Escape closes it.

The game uses a bundled English dictionary with an offensive-word filter. Proper names and abbreviations are not intended; dictionary coverage may be imperfect. New players arriving mid-round watch until the next lobby.

Score = letter values + 2 × (word length − 3)². Dictionary & software credits ↗
