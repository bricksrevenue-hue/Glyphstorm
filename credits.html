Credits & dictionary

Original orbit. Open-source foundations.

Glyphstorm's interface, ship and crystal drawings, light effects, and synthesized sounds were created for this game. It uses HTML5 Canvas, vanilla JavaScript, Node.js, Express, and Socket.io.

Which words count?

The server checks a bundled English word list rather than asking an AI or a live dictionary API during play. Words must contain 3–10 alphabetic letters and use available tray tiles. A word may score once per player per round. Proper names and abbreviations are not intended; dictionary coverage is imperfect, and some uncommon words may be accepted.

The dictionary is provided by word-list, based on atebits/Words, with an additional exact-word filter using bad-words. No automated filter catches every inappropriate term.

Libraries: Express, Socket.io, word-list, bad-words, and their dependencies. Their license notices are available in Third-party notices.

Privacy & sessions

No account is required to play. Your guest name, tray, and round state are held in server memory while the room is active. Your browser stores a private session token to reconnect, plus optional name, sound, and motion preferences. Room data is temporary; a server restart ends existing rooms. Hosting providers may retain ordinary access logs.

Use a nickname rather than a full name. Share room codes only with people you want to play with.
